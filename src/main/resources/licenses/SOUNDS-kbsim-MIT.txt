Hazeline keyboard sounds (assets/hazeline/sounds/kb/<pack>/) are converted from the recordings in
kbsim - Mechanical Keyboard Simulator by Thomas Lai:
  https://github.com/tplai/kbsim  (src/assets/audio, MIT licence below)
  clicky <- mxblue (Cherry MX Blue)      creamy    <- cream (NovelKeys Cream)
  alps_blue <- bluealps (SKCM Blue Alps) box_navy  <- boxnavy (Kailh Box Navy)
  buckling <- buckling (Buckling spring) alpaca    <- alpaca
  black_ink <- blackink (Gateron Black Ink) tealios <- turquoise (Turquoise Tealios)
Edits: mono, leading silence removed, gain normalised (peak -3 dBFS), re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis
(script: tools/sounds/make_packs.py in the Hazeline source).
The Clicky/Creamy GUI sounds (sounds/clicky, sounds/creamy) were edited from Mechvibes' mxblue-travel and
cream-travel folders, which are byte-identical copies of these kbsim recordings (checked 2026-10-24 by MD5),
so this notice applies to them as well (see also SOUNDS-Mechvibes-MIT.txt).
